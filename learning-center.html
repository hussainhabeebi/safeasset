Plain language guides to help Kerala families understand health insurance clearly — before they need it.

What is a Family Floater Plan?

A family floater covers your entire family under one sum insured. More affordable than individual plans for each member. Best when all members are below 55 and generally healthy.

⏳

Understanding Waiting Periods

30 days: Initial waiting. Accidents covered from day 1.
2–4 years: Pre-existing diseases.
9 months–2 years: Maternity.

The key insight: waiting periods begin only after you buy. Buying early means completing them before you need the coverage.

🏥

Cashless vs Reimbursement

Cashless: Go to a network hospital. Show health card. Insurer pays hospital directly. You pay only non-covered items.

Reimbursement: Get treated anywhere. Pay first. Submit documents. Insurer reimburses eligible expenses within 30–45 days.

👴

Health Insurance for Senior Parents

Plans available for ages 60–80+. Key insurers: Niva Bupa Senior First, Star Senior Red Carpet (no medicals up to 75), Care Senior.

Buy before health conditions develop — more options available and premiums are lower at younger ages.

📊

Section 80D Tax Benefits

Health insurance premiums are tax deductible under Section 80D:

Self + family: ₹25,000/year
Senior parents: ₹50,000 additional
Total possible: ₹75,000/year

At 30% tax bracket — ₹22,500 saved in taxes annually.

🔄

Porting Your Health Insurance

You can switch insurer at renewal without losing waiting period credits (IRDAI mandated portability). No-claim bonus also transfers.

Apply 45 days before renewal. Cannot port mid-term.

Useful if you are not satisfied with current insurer's service or network.
