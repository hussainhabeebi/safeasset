Rasi's Story

From Land Dealer
To Savings Protector

I started my career as a land dealer. Over the years, I met many families who worked hard to build a home, buy land, save money, and secure their children's future.

But I also witnessed something painful.

Families selling property, gold, and savings to manage medical expenses.

That experience changed my perspective. I realised that protecting assets is not only about buying property. It is also about protecting what you have already built.

That is why I started SafeAssets.

Today my mission is simple: Help families understand health insurance before they need it. Not after.
