Insurance Is Purchased
While You Are Healthy.

Not because you are sick. Understanding this one truth can protect everything your family has built.

⏳

Why Families Delay

  • "I am healthy now"
  • "I will take it later"
  • "Insurance is expensive"
  • "Claims are difficult"
  • "I don't understand the plans"
Each of these reasons feels logical — until the day you actually need a policy that isn't there.
⚠️

Common Mistakes

✗
Waiting too long

Pre-existing diseases accumulate. Waiting periods begin only after purchase.

✗
Choosing only by premium

The cheapest plan often has the biggest gaps. Coverage matters more than cost.

✗
Ignoring parents

Parent hospitalisation is the most common reason families drain savings.

✗
Not understanding coverage

Discovering exclusions during hospitalisation is a painful lesson.

✗
Treating it as an expense

It is protection. Like a seatbelt — you hope you never need it, but it must be there.

The Truth

The Best Time Was Yesterday.
The Second Best Time Is Today.

Every year you wait: premiums increase with age, more pre-existing conditions develop, waiting periods start later, and your savings are at greater risk.

30s
Buy now. Lowest premium. No pre-existing issues yet. Waiting periods complete early.
40s
Still good options. Premium higher. Cover parents too while they are younger and healthier.
50s+
Plans still available but at higher cost. Getting started now is still far better than not at all.
