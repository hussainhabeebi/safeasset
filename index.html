When should I buy health insurance?
The best time is when you are healthy. Many benefits have waiting periods — buying early gives you access to protection before you need it.
Can I buy health insurance for my parents?
Yes. Many plans allow you to cover parents. Available options depend on age, current health condition, and medical history. The earlier you explore, the more options are available.
What is the difference between cashless and reimbursement claims?
Cashless: insurer settles directly with the hospital. Reimbursement: you pay first, then claim eligible expenses by submitting documents.
How much coverage do I need?
It depends on your family size, age of members, and existing conditions. Rasi helps families understand what level of protection suits their specific situation.
